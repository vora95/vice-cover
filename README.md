Vice Cover turns your photos and a caption into a multi-panel sunset cover poster — hero photo in the centre, satellite crops and procedural skyline/palm art around it, bold gradient lettering on top — and exports it as a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080. Three themes (Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour), two layouts, drag-to-pan cropping, and a fully procedural demo scene so it looks like a poster before you upload anything. Everything renders offline in the browser with vanilla JavaScript and Canvas 2D; photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8000 --directory app
```

Then open http://localhost:8000/ in a desktop browser. Or open `app/index.html` directly from disk — the app works from `file://` too.
